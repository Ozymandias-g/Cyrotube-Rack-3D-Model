# 3D Printed Cryotube Rack 🧪

My teacher needed a custom rack to hold some specific lab sample tubes, so I designed and 3D printed this one from scratch. 

## How I Made It
* **Measuring:** I used a digital caliper to get the exact outer diameters and lengths of the sample tubes to ensure they fit snugly without rattling around.
* **Modeling:** I built the 3D model in **SolidWorks**. You can grab my original part file, `CyroTubeRack_v1.SLDPRT`[cite: 13], if you need to adjust the hole tolerances for different tube sizes.
* **Printing:** I sliced the exported model and 3D printed the final piece for the lab.

## What's in this Repo?
* `CyroTubeRack_v1.SLDPRT`: The native SolidWorks design file[cite: 13].
* `Print_Files/`: The exported `.STL` files ready to drop straight into your slicer.
* `Photos/`: A few shots of the printed rack holding the actual tubes.
